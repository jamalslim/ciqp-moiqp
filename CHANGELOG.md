# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release, accompanying the manuscript.

Contents are the classical side of the work: the Van den Nest correlator
estimator, the Pearson-Stabilized Correlation Kernel, Mixture-of-IQP training,
the exact Walsh-Hadamard compilation of the mixture into a single IQP circuit,
the degree-3 Walsh-sparse form, and the structural audits. The five headline
training runs are included, so the tables can be checked without retraining.

Everything runs on CPU. There is no quantum-SDK dependency.
